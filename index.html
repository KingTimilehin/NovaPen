FAQ

INTEL
BRIEFING.

Is there a free trial for the Operative plan? +
Affirmative. Every Operative account initialises with a 14-day free trial. No card required. You only get billed if you choose to continue after trial completion.
Does NovaPen run offline? +
AI modules require a live connection. However, all drafts are cached locally — you can keep writing offline and the system auto-syncs when back online.
What export formats are supported? +
Markdown (.md), HTML, plain text, and PDF. Operative and Command users get direct pipeline integrations with GitHub, Notion, and WordPress.
How does team collaboration work? +
Command tier unlocks a shared workspace: real-time co-editing, inline comments, and full version history for every document across all operators.
Can I abort my subscription at any time? +
Confirmed. Cancel from account settings at any point. Access remains active through end of billing cycle. All documents remain yours to export at any time.
